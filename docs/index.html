      Release mode

      These apply whatever you choose

      • Hard rules (approved suppliers, certifications, minimum order quantities, delivery-date arithmetic, the circuit-board freeze) run before any order exists. Nothing here can release an order that breaks one.
      • The policy's own approvals always hold an order: over $40,000 (Sourcing Manager), over $120,000 (VP of Supply Chain), any air freight, and moving business away from a Strategic supplier.
      • Settings decide whether an order waits, not who approves it. The approver comes from the order's value and the policy.
