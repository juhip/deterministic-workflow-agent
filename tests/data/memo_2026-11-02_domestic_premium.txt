HARBOR FABRICATION CO. - OPERATIONS MEMO
Memo ID: MEMO-2026-090
Date: November 2, 2026
From: Dana Whitcombe, VP of Supply Chain
To: Purchasing
Subject: Wider domestic preference for critical parts

Because of continuing port congestion, effective November 2, 2026 the domestic price premium threshold for critical
parts is raised from 45% to 65% of the international price. For other parts the threshold stays at 30%. This
replaces the critical-part threshold in Purchasing Policy Section 3.
Please also consider whether lead-time guarantees belong in supplier contracts.
